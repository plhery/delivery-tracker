# Carrier color references

Reviewed 2026-09-12. These are compact UI accents drawn from carrier logos and
website palettes, not a claim to reproduce every official print specification.
Exact SVG fills or website colors were used where available; approximate
choices are called out below. The carrier READMEs in this checkout contain no
logos. No remote logo assets are bundled or fetched by the app.

`carriers/<id>/carrier.json` remains the editable source of truth. Only `unknown`
keeps the neutral gray. Yellow trucks use dark labels and decorations for readability; other text and
surface colors use the shared light/dark palette derivation.

## Custom decorations

These are small, simplified brand-inspired marks on the shared truck, not full
wordmark reproductions: [FedEx](https://www.fedex.com/) has an orange forward
arrow on purple, [DPD](https://www.dpd.com/) a white parcel cube on red,
[Amazon](https://shipping.amazon.com/) an orange smile on a dark truck, and
[Japan Post](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/) the white postal mark on red.
DHL eCommerce shares DHL's existing stripes; DPD France and Amazon Shipping
share their respective families' decorations. The geometry lives in `truck.json`.

## Additional carrier decorations

These marks simplify recognizable brand elements to work in a 27 × 18 pixel
truck. They are drawn locally from polygons, line segments and circles, and
use the same data on web and iPhone. References reviewed 2026-09-12:

| Carrier | Decoration and palette reference |
| --- | --- |
| Swiss Post | Red Swiss cross and black P on yellow, based on the [current Swiss Post identity](https://site.post.ch/en/about-us/company/the-brand-swiss-post). Shared with Swiss Post Cargo and PostLogistics. |
| Quickpac | A white Q with a forward tail and speed lines. Keeps the distinct Quickpac name for historical parcels, using the existing red palette; [Quickpac's site](https://quickpac.ch/) now presents Quickmail/Planzer branding. |
| La Poste | The three bird contours from the official SVG, uniformly scaled at their original 54:24 aspect ratio on yellow; blue `#003da5` from the [website SVG](https://www.laposte.fr/ecom/_nuxt/logo-part-horizontal.BtezbiGr.svg). |
| Chronopost | A cyan parcel cube with a darker left face and the original tall 76:89 silhouette on a white truck for contrast, traced from the [official Chronopost guide cover](https://static.chronopost.fr/pdf/chronopost/guide_utilisateur_Shopify.pdf). |
| India Post | Two gold flight lines on red, inspired by the [official logo](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/images/home/logo/indiapostlogo.svg); gold `#fedb01` comes from that SVG. |
| Mondial Relay | A nearly square burgundy badge containing the white person and pink parcel, traced from the [red badge and pink visual identity](https://storage.mondialrelay.fr/Mondial%20Relay%20transforme%20sa%20marque%20avec%20Dragon%20Rouge.pdf). Colors are sampled UI approximations. |
| PostNL (`spring-gds`) | A white crown on the existing orange truck, inspired by the [official PostNL media kit](https://newsroom.postnl.nl/en-NL/assets/237879/). The historical carrier ID remains `spring-gds`, but the catalog identifies this service as PostNL. |

The La Poste, Chronopost and Mondial Relay silhouettes preserve their reference aspect ratios. Curves are flattened
to short polygon edges so the web SVG and native Canvas share the same outline.

The remaining compact marks also get optical adjustments: a larger, centered UPS
shield with a gold rim, a smoother tapered Amazon smile and India Post flight
lines, and lighter FedEx, Quickpac and PostNL details. These remain simplified
brand-inspired decorations rather than miniature wordmarks. UPS now shares the
same polygon outline on web and iPhone, including its curved shoulders and tip.
