Delivery Tracker

Privacy notice

Effective 12 September 2026

Delivery Tracker processes information to authenticate you, follow parcel deliveries, power the optional Friends circle and diagnose failures. It does not sell personal data, serve ads, or use advertising analytics. It uses optional, self-hosted usage analytics.

What is processed

  • Your email address, account ID, session metadata, authentication events, and basic profile data when you choose Google sign-in.
  • Parcel labels, tracking numbers, carriers, status, tracking history, operational scan locations, sender or business names, pickup-point details, parcel weight and dimensions where the carrier provides them, supplied delivery postcodes, and combined tracking credentials that can contain a delivery postcode.
  • Optional Planzer shared and Dachser Customer Iberia capability links.
  • Push endpoints, encryption keys, browser user agent, native APNs device and Live Activity tokens, device settings, delivery acknowledgements and errors when you enable notifications or Live Activities.
  • Network metadata processed by the hosting, proxy, authentication, and email infrastructure, such as IP address, timestamp, and user agent.
  • Diagnostic logs and Sentry error reports include tracking numbers, synchronization outcomes and error details. Sentry may retain original request/response headers, URLs and bounded body excerpts, including personal data and session or authentication information present in those diagnostics. No field-based redaction is applied to these reports.

How it is used

Supabase provides authentication and database access. Google provides social sign-in, and the configured SMTP provider sends codes when email OTP is enabled. Cloudflare and the container host may process network metadata. The selected carrier receives its tracking number or carrier-specific tracking credential so Delivery Tracker can retrieve updates. Fallback providers (Ship24, ParcelsApp and 17TRACK, plus Postal Ninja when enabled) receive the tracking number when used. ParcelsApp additionally receives the stored delivery postcode when one is supplied. DPD Switzerland may also receive the parcel's supplied postcode for recipient verification. Mondial Relay requires the five-digit recipient postcode to retrieve shipment events. Colis Privé receives a combined credential made from its 12-character shipment number and the five-digit delivery postcode. Planzer receives a shared-link capability when you provide one. Dachser receives its supplied capability link; sender, recipient, address, contact, and document fields in Dachser's response are discarded. Web Push endpoints receive encrypted notifications you requested. Apple processes native notifications and Live Activity updates through APNs; these can contain parcel labels, carrier, status, location and estimated delivery text, but the app does not include tracking numbers in those Apple payloads.

Usage analytics

The official public website and connected iPhone app send screen views and named feature actions, including success or failure, to our self-hosted Umami at u.plhery.com. Events include platform, demo/account/anonymous mode, language and basic browser/device information. Umami processes IP address and user agent to derive approximate location and rotating visitor/session identifiers. This helps us improve features and find failing actions.

We do not send account IDs, emails, parcel contents, labels, tracking numbers, delivery postcodes, invitation codes, search text, full URLs, referrers or error messages. No analytics cookies or persistent analytics device identifiers are created, and events are not linked to your account.

Turn off Usage analytics in Account on each device to stop collection. The website also honors Do Not Track, Global Privacy Control and Umami's local opt-out. Opting out discards queued events. Historical analytics remain until operational cleanup; they cannot be selected by account for export or deletion because no account identifier is sent. Local demo builds, simulators and unconfigured self-hosted instances do not collect.

Friends is optional

Friends uses a nickname you choose, separate from your sign-in profile. There is no public directory or contact upload. Only people you connect with through a single-use invitation can see your friend card. Invitations expire after seven days. Current invitation keys are stored in an access-restricted table and removed when accepted or revoked. Anyone holding a link can preview your nickname and choose to accept after signing in; the link is an invitation credential. Older invitation formats use a separately hashed acceptance token.

You choose whether to share your delivered-parcel count, average journey rounded up to whole days, and earned Passport stamps. A separate setting, off by default, can show that a parcel arrived during the current week. Friends never receive parcel contents, labels, tracking numbers, carriers, locations, exact delivery times, or information about active shipments. Your email, Google name and photo are not part of your friend card.

Preview your card before saving. Changes apply to the next refresh, at most a minute while Friends is open. Friend cards are not saved for offline use. Removing a connection revokes access on both sides; turning off Friends deletes your friend profile, connections and invitations. Anyone who has already seen a card could still have kept a screenshot or copy.

Retention and your controls

Parcel data remains until you delete the account. Archiving hides a parcel from the active list but retains its history. Infrastructure backups and security logs may remain for their operator's limited retention period. Diagnostic logs and Sentry reports can retain tracking numbers after parcel or account deletion until their configured retention expires. Browser notification registrations and delivery records may remain until account deletion or operational cleanup.

Use Download my data in the account menu for a JSON export, including your Friends profile and connections. Use Delete account to permanently remove your Auth user, parcels, events, push subscriptions, Friends profile, connections and invitations. Signing out clears account-scoped local state, including the offline parcel snapshot; browser controls can clear the remaining PWA cache and notification permission.

Security and contact

The service uses HTTPS, short-lived access tokens, rotating refresh tokens, Postgres row-level security, account rate limits, and server-only secrets. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Report a privacy or security concern through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting. Never put a real tracking number or combined tracking credential, delivery postcode, sign-in code, token, Planzer shared link, or Dachser detail link in a public issue.

This notice covers the official Delivery Tracker service for parcel tracking. Third-party forks are operated independently and must publish their own notice.
