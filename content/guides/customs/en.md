"Held at customs" usually means your parcel (or package) is in the routine import check for goods from abroad, not that it was seized. If it stays held, the carrier may be waiting for you to pay a charge or send an invoice. Check the carrier's own website or app, never a link in a text.

## What does "held at customs" mean?

Every parcel crossing a customs border is presented to customs: by law, Royal Mail presents every item from outside the UK to Border Force, and Canada Post presents every incoming international parcel to the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA). In Ireland, that means parcels from outside the EU, Great Britain included.

Customs checks the sender's declaration (contents, value), may open the parcel, works out any tax and duty, then releases it to the carrier. Carriers word this stage differently:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `In customs` (Royal Mail) | Passing through customs |
| `OE` in a Royal Mail scan | Office of Exchange: it reached the import customs office |
| `Fee to pay` or `Held with fee to pay` (Royal Mail) | A customs charge (or missing postage) must be paid before delivery |
| `Item held by customs` (Canada Post) | Standard for every international parcel, says Canada Post |

None of these is a seizure. A real one comes in writing: Ireland's Revenue says you get a written notification with the customs office's details, and Australian Border Force (ABF) sends an information sheet saying what it holds and why.

## What to do if your parcel is held at customs

Often nothing. To find out:

:::steps
- Open the carrier's own tracking | Type its address or open its app, never a link from a text or email.
- Look for a request | A `Fee to pay` status, a Pay button or a request for documents means it's waiting for you. Otherwise, nothing to do yet.
- Do what it asks | Pay on its own site or at the counter, or send the invoice or order confirmation it wants.
- Ask the carrier, then the seller | Still stuck? Revenue sends you to the courier, postal operator or seller about delays, not to customs.
:::

### How do I contact customs about a package?

Mostly you don't: start with the carrier (step 4). In Canada, even Canada Post can't intervene while the CBSA reviews a parcel, so you wait for release. In Australia, ask Australia Post (13 13 18) about delivery and ABF about an Import Declaration or a payment.

## How long can a parcel be held at customs?

Nobody promises a time. The CBSA releases most mail after an initial inspection, which "can take less than a day". Canada Post says times vary with the parcel, its paperwork and the volume at customs. For a YunExpress parcel from China, see [how long YunExpress says a customs check adds](carrier:yunexpress).

A parcel stays longer when:

- the declaration lacks data, or customs wants the invoice or order confirmation (Revenue);
- customs doesn't accept the declared value (ABF);
- a charge is unpaid: Canada Post and couriers in Canada hold the parcel until you pay;
- the goods may be restricted: the CBSA inspects again or calls in an agency such as Health Canada.

Ordering Christmas gifts from abroad? [Leave time for customs](guide:christmas-posting-dates) and order early.

Don't leave it too long:

| Country | How long it waits |
| --- | --- |
| UK | Royal Mail, Parcelforce or the courier "normally" hold it about 3 weeks, then return it |
| Ireland | An Post asks you to pay within 10 working days |
| Australia | Over AUD 1,000: with no action 30 days after Australia Post's First Notice, it may go back |
| US | A mail parcel without the required entry or bond stays with U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) until they are filed |

Tired of refreshing? Paste the number into [Peek](/): it checks up to every 10 minutes and notifies you when the status changes, though it can't pay the charge or hurry customs. If nothing moves for long and no request appears, see [why tracking stops updating](guide:tracking-not-updating).

### How long after customs clearance will I get it?

"Released" or "cleared" means customs has handed the parcel back to the carrier, which then updates tracking and delivers as quickly as possible, says Canada Post. For the scans that follow, see [what tracking statuses mean](guide:tracking-statuses).

## Why am I getting a customs charge?

Usually because tax or duty on goods from abroad wasn't paid at checkout, so the carrier collects it, often adding its own fee. The bill has up to three parts:

1. **Import tax**: VAT in the UK and Ireland, GST/HST in Canada, GST in Australia, owed to the state.
2. **Customs duty**: the tariff on the goods, also for the state.
3. **The carrier's fee** for clearing the parcel: a service charge, not a tax. CBP says the "customs fees" couriers bill are "actually charges for the services of the broker".

Where you pay depends on the checkout:

:::journey
- shop | Checkout | Tax, sometimes duty, paid here if the shop collects it
- plane | Flight | Travels with its customs declaration
- customs | Customs | Checks it and may ask for an invoice
- handover | Carrier | Bills you anything unpaid, plus its own fee
- home | You | Delivered once the bill is paid
:::

| Country | Free at the door if | Otherwise you pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UK | Goods you bought for £135 or less (the seller adds VAT), or a gift worth £39 or less. Never alcohol, tobacco or vapes: they pay duty at any value | VAT, plus duty above £135. Royal Mail adds £8, Parcelforce £12, both £25 for goods over £900; couriers set their own |
| Ireland | The shop charged it all at checkout, or a gift worth €45 or less | VAT and duty, even on goods from Great Britain, plus the carrier's fee (An Post: €6.95) |
| Canada | Worth CAD 20 or less (CAD 40 by courier from the US or Mexico), or a gift up to CAD 60 | Duty and sales taxes. Canada Post adds CAD 9.95, only when something is owed |
| Australia | Worth AUD 1,000 or less (the shop may have charged GST), except alcohol and tobacco | Over AUD 1,000: an Import Declaration, then duty and taxes to ABF |
| US | Only a gift worth $100 or less: the $800 duty-free limit has been suspended since 29 August 2025 | Duty. A courier's broker bills it with its fees; a mail parcel needs an entry filed by its buyer, owner or a licensed broker |

### Ireland: what "€3 per item" means

Since 1 July 2026, online orders from outside the EU worth €150 or less pay €3 duty per item, meaning per kind of product:

- two identical T-shirts: €3;
- a notepad, a pen and a keyring: €9.

From 1 November 2026, Revenue says a €2 EU handling fee per item is added: €15 for the notepad, pen and keyring. VAT comes on top. Above €150, normal duty rates apply, plus the €2 fee.

## Do I have to pay customs on Temu or Shein orders?

It depends on your country and the checkout:

- **UK:** nothing at the door up to £135; the seller adds VAT to your price.
- **Ireland:** if the checkout showed VAT and duty, "the total price you pay at checkout will be the final price", says Revenue; if not, the carrier collects them first.
- **Canada:** above CAD 20 by post, duty and taxes apply wherever you shop.
- **Australia:** nothing at the border up to AUD 1,000; the shop may have charged GST.
- **US:** with the $800 duty-free limit suspended, duty (tariffs) can apply to cheap orders too, and CBP warns that a price including shipping and handling doesn't include it.

For how these orders travel, see [tracking a parcel from China](guide:tracking-from-china).

## How do I know if a customs charge text is real?

Real charges can come by text or email too, but each carrier's rules give most fakes away:

| Carrier | What its real messages look like |
| --- | --- |
| Royal Mail, Parcelforce | A `Fee to pay` card, letter, SMS or email, whose details you enter on Royal Mail's "Pay a fee" page |
| An Post | Post, text or email, but "never" a message containing any links |
| USPS | Texts or emails only if you asked for tracking with your number, and never with a link |
| Canada Post | The E14 form on the parcel, or the Pay button in its own tracking or app |
| Australia Post | It never calls, texts or emails you to request payment |

Customs authorities warn too:

- **Revenue** (Ireland) never emails or texts you to click and pay customs duty.
- **The CBSA** never initiates a request for your Social Insurance Number or card number by phone, text or email; a call demanding duty with threats of jail is a scam.
- **ABF** warns of scam calls that seem to come from its Regional Office numbers.

Safest is step 1 above: open the carrier's site or app yourself. The same goes for [any delivery text you didn't ask for](guide:universal-tracker).

> Tapped the link and typed your card details? Call your bank now, even if you never pressed submit, as the U.S. Postal Inspection Service advises.

To report a fake:

- **UK:** forward texts to 7726 (free) and emails to report@phishing.gov.uk.
- **US:** forward texts to 7726, and USPS-themed ones to spam@uspis.gov with a screenshot.
- **Canada:** the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre.
- **Australia:** scams@auspost.com.au. If you've been caught, iDCare on 1800 595 160.

## What if I don't pay, or send it back?

In the UK and Ireland, an unpaid parcel goes back to the sender after the waiting times above. In Australia, a parcel over AUD 1,000 may go back if you take no action within 30 days of the First Notice. Refunds and disputes:

- **UK:** reclaim VAT or duty after a return or overcharge with form BOR 286 (Royal Mail, Parcelforce) or C285 (other couriers).
- **Canada:** ask for a review before paying (the parcel waits), or pay and claim afterwards, as the E14 explains.
- **Ireland:** the €3 duty is refunded only on faulty goods, and the €2 fee never.
