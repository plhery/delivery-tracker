# Help with Peek

Peek follows parcels, on the web at [peektracker.com](https://peektracker.com)
and in its iPhone app. It shows what carriers publish about a parcel.

Peek is not a carrier. It cannot redirect, hold or find a parcel. For that, ask
the seller or the carrier. [Peek's guides](https://peektracker.com/guides) say
who to ask when a parcel is late, held at customs or marked delivered but
missing.

## A parcel shows nothing, or looks wrong

- Nothing yet: a carrier often knows a parcel only a day or two after the
  order. Keep the page. Peek keeps asking, and shows the parcel as soon as a
  carrier answers.
- Compare the number with the shipping message. One wrong character is enough.
- Some carriers also need the delivery postcode or the complete tracking link.
  Peek asks for it when a carrier does.
- Wrong carrier: signed in, open the parcel's ••• menu and choose
  **Change carrier**.
- The parcel's page links to the carrier's own website. When that site says the
  same as Peek, the carrier has the answer.

Still wrong? Below a parcel's history, Pip, the parcel with a face, asks
**Did I get this one right?** Choose **Not quite**, pick what is off, and add a
note if you like. When no carrier was found, the page asks whether the
carrier's own site shows the parcel, and lets you name the carrier. A person
reads the answers. An answer carries the parcel's number and nothing about
you, so nobody can write back: for a reply, use the address under Contact.

## The sign-in code did not arrive

Peek has no passwords. It emails a six-digit code to the address you enter.

- Look in the spam folder.
- Check the address. **Use a different email** goes back to correct it.
- Wait a minute, then choose **Email me a code** again. An expired code is
  refused: ask for a new one.
- Where the sign-in screen offers **Continue with Google**, a Google address
  opens the same account without a code.

## Notifications

- In a browser, a parcel's page has **Notify me**. It needs no account and
  stops when the parcel is delivered. Signed in, Settings › Notifications
  covers every parcel in the account.
- In a browser on an iPhone, notifications only come from a site added to the
  Home Screen. Peek shows the steps. Then open Peek from the Home Screen and
  turn notifications on there.
- In the iPhone app, notifications need an account. Sign in, then open
  Settings › Notifications.
- Nothing arrives? Check that Peek may send notifications: in the browser's
  site settings, or in iPhone Settings.
- Peek announces only the updates you chose: every scan, the important steps
  or delivery day only.
- Signed in, Settings › Notifications also has
  **Email me when a parcel arrives**.

## Your data

- A copy: Settings › Account & data › **Download my data** saves a file with
  the account, its parcels and their history.
- One parcel: open its ••• menu and choose **Delete permanently**.
  **Archive parcel** only hides it and keeps its history.
- The account: Settings › Account & data › **Delete account** erases it with
  its parcels, their history and its notification settings. Peek asks for the
  account's email address to confirm, and for a new sign-in when the last one
  is more than ten minutes old.
- Without an account, the browser a parcel was tracked from can forget it:
  **Forget it now**, at the foot of the parcel's page. In the iPhone app,
  Settings › On this device has **Forget all**. Peek forgets such a parcel by
  itself 30 days after delivery.

The [privacy notice](https://peektracker.com/privacy.html) says what Peek
keeps, where and for how long.
