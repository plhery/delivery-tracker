Good cards need a good home.

The Fleet marks, soft cards, and postcard stay. Three explorations give them a clearer rhythm, a better use of width, and a quieter place for past deliveries.

Based on a review of the current app.
These are interactive layout studies, using fictional parcels.

01 / Less space before the parcels

Search and refresh share the section heading. A slightly shorter postcard keeps more deliveries in the first screen, without shrinking the text or the tap targets.

02 / Give “Next up” a clear meaning

The featured parcel is the next arrival or pickup. A customs issue has its own small notice. Unknown dates never become the main headline.

03 / Keep the past in perspective

Recent deliveries use softer surfaces or a compact journal. They stay latest first, with the count beside the heading. Archived parcels stay tucked underneath.
