Ask when it makes sense

After the first real parcel is saved and its form closes. For existing users, on their next deliveries visit. Skip empty accounts and demo mode.

Remember the answer

“Not now” dismisses the invitation in this browser. Never repeat it automatically after denial or an explicit switch-off. Settings remains available.

Let the browser ask
